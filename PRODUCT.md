## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, deployed as a static site on free GitHub Pages through GitHub Actions. The repository is already published. The user reviews local changes and handles commits and pushes unless they explicitly request the assistant to do so.

## Users

People considering psychotherapy with Mag. Romina Lauzensky in Vienna or Mödling, including people seeking individual, couples, or family therapy. Their likely task is to understand her approach and practical terms before making contact; this task description is a design inference from the existing site.

## Product Purpose

Present Romina Lauzensky, her therapeutic offers, approach, process, practice locations, and contact paths. The primary visitor action is to contact the practice. This action is inferred from the current homepage and contact page.

## Positioning

The current site describes a person-centered therapeutic relationship, with conversation and, where useful, creative methods, imagination, meditative elements, and Brainspotting. It also lists animal-assisted psychotherapy with alpacas and artistic and experiential elements as distinct offers. Preserve these as the practitioner's own descriptions; do not add clinical claims.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Redesign the full existing website, preserving its page structure, menu destinations, and all existing images. The visual style and menu presentation may change.
- German is the primary language; add complete English equivalents and an easy language switch to the equivalent page.
- Preserve public German URLs where feasible, and account for older URLs that remain indexed.
- The current site includes a contact form. The user chose a local “not yet available” notice on submit; no form data is sent or stored. Direct email and phone links work.
- The repository is https://github.com/maxwilsonpereira/romina-website. GitHub Actions deploys it to GitHub Pages after a push. Leave new work local for review; do not commit, push, update the remote branch, or deploy unless the user explicitly requests that action.
- Light German copy editing is allowed if every factual claim and page section remains faithful to the source.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name and professional title presented by the site: Mag. Romina Lauzensky, Psychotherapeutin.
- Keep the existing photographs and other imagery; do not replace them with synthetic depictions of Romina, her practice, or clients.
- Create a beautiful modern atmosphere appropriate to psychotherapy. The specific visual direction remains for the user to choose from a board.
- Avoid a trendy or playful tone.

## Product Principles

1. Make it easy to understand the therapist's approach and decide whether to reach out.
2. Preserve factual accuracy and the existing public content structure.
3. Make both languages equally useful while keeping German as the default.
