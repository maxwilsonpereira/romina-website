# Homepage surface brief

## Scope and mode

Redesign the homepage as the entry to the complete bilingual practice site. Mode: Persuade.

## Visitor and task

A person considering psychotherapy in Vienna or Mödling should understand who Romina is, what she offers, and how to contact her. The original portrait and practice photograph are evidence. The contact form and direct email/phone are the action.

## Constraints

German first, English equivalent; preserve the current page destinations and all original photos. Avoid playful or trendy treatment. Claims, legal details, and availability stay within verified source content. The static host needs a form submission solution.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a quiet threshold into a real practice, with Romina's presence and a first step to contact visible immediately. It refuses the interchangeable portrait-beside-cards therapy template.

OWN-WORLD: Warm plaster, soft daylight, deep green-gray ink, celadon from the actual chair, one muted terracotta signal; humanist serif display and clear sans body type. Openings, reveals, and calm architectural framing organize navigation, fields, and long content.

STORY: Meet Romina and her room, understand the person-centered approach, explore the six offers and practical terms, then contact the practice with confidence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A tall architectural frame holds the unchanged original practice-room photograph as the dominant left-hand arrival. The welcome statement and a generous contact area occupy the right-hand plaster surface. Main menu and DE/EN switch span the top; name, email, message, action, and direct contact details are visible before scrolling. The therapeutic offer begins at the bottom edge. On mobile, the framed photograph and text stack while the form stays generous and readable.

FORM: Architectural threshold, third of seven grounded candidates; selected on the Impeccable direction board. Seed reference: seed:5f43cc70b9063f1d. Approved composition: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-2-doorway.png`. The photographed doorway in the preview is conceptual; recreate the frame in CSS around the original source image, without adding or changing photography.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
